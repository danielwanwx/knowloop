# Adaptive follow-ups

Choose the next question from the last answer, not a fixed parade of personas.

1. Identify a concrete claim or missing causal link. If it is correct and justified, move on; don't manufacture a flaw to sound strict.
2. Probe one level deeper: “what actually happens?”, “why that choice?”, “how do you know?”, “what would break?”, or “what changes under this condition?”
3. Wait. Distinguish absent knowledge, missing detail, misunderstood prompt, speech transcription error and language trouble. Clarify a likely transcription mistake before treating it as conceptual failure.
4. In Practice/Learn, help one level at a time; log hints in order. In Mock, keep the interview moving and defer teaching unless requested.
5. Repair one mechanism, then have the learner reconstruct it. Only a genuinely unseen problem can produce a new H0 attempt after teaching. Returning the same answer in another language does not erase hints.

## Examples of changing the test, not merely rewording it

- Algorithm: the first solution assumes all input fits in memory; now explain processing a stream. Ask for an implementation only after the new invariant is clear.
- SQL/data: a join worked with one row per customer; introduce repeated events or nulls and ask how the result changes.
- System design/fundamentals: after a correct single-worker explanation, introduce concurrency, a timeout, skew or failure. Ask for the first failure and the guarantee, not a technology shopping list.
- Resume/project: after “we improved latency,” ask who measured the baseline, which part the learner changed, and how they ruled out another cause. Do not turn missing measurements into invented percentages.
- Behavioral: after a conflict story, ask what the other person wanted and what the learner would change. Alternative situations are hypothetical, not additional personal history.
- Introduction: change the interviewer from a backend engineer to a hiring manager and ask for a concise version emphasizing the same verified experience.

## Repair material

Provide one short correction card in the saved evaluation: the supported gap, the mechanism, a concrete example, optional English phrasing, one accurate source/section if needed, and next-check target. Explain which parts are coach-generated. Retest targets are capabilities, not a hidden answer key in the learner's notes.

No mock-style imitation can substitute for technical checks. Interviewer personas are optional variations in questioning behavior, not a claim of hiring realism or a guarantee of interview success.
